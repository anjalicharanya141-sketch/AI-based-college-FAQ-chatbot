Q: How does the chatbot work?
A: The AI Based College FAQ Chatbot uses natural language processing, semantic search, vector embeddings and generative AI to answer questions from the college knowledge base.

Q: What AI technology is used?
A: The chatbot uses a sentence-transformer model for creating text embeddings, ChromaDB for similarity search, and Google's Gemini generative AI model for generating natural-language answers.

Q: Can the chatbot answer questions that are not in the FAQ?
A: The chatbot is designed to answer questions using the information available in its knowledge base. If relevant information is not available, it should indicate that the information could not be found.

Q: Is the chatbot available 24 hours?
A: The software application can be run whenever the system and required services are available. Actual availability depends on how the application is deployed.
